Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026. This policy explains how Arcanelle (“the app”), provided by Ha Cong Hau (Hà Công Hậu) (“we”), handles your information.

Short version: the app works without an account. Your readings, notes and settings are stored on your device. We do not collect, sell or share personal data, and the app contains no advertising or tracking.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Readings you draw, the question you type, your journal notes and mood, and your preferences.
  • This data stays on your device. If iCloud is turned on for the app on your device, it is also synced in your private iCloud account by Apple, which we cannot access.

2. Information we collect

None by default. The app does not send your notes, mood or journal to us. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking. The only exception is cloud AI reflections, which you turn on yourself (section 5).

3. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple shares with us only anonymous transaction information needed to confirm your subscription.

4. Notifications

If you allow reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

5. AI reflections

  • On-device AI. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, reflections are written by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Cloud AI (only if you allow it). On other iPhones, and only after you tap “Allow cloud AI”, the cards of a reading, their positions, the topic and your question are sent to our server and processed with Cloudflare Workers AI to write the reflection. Your name, Apple ID, notes, mood and location are not sent.
  • To check a Premium subscription, our server sends Apple the anonymous original transaction ID of your purchase. Free trials are counted with a random installation ID that is not linked to you.
  • We do not store or log the content of readings or questions, and they are not used to train AI models. Our server keeps only usage counters (for quotas): a hashed installation ID for up to 13 months, a hashed transaction ID for up to 40 days and a hashed IP address for up to 2 days (to prevent abuse).
  • You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings › AI reflections. AI reflections are generated automatically, may be inaccurate and are not predictions or professional advice.

5a. Legal bases, service providers and transfers (EU/EEA/UK)

  • Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6): cloud AI reflections are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time; checking a subscription is necessary to provide the service you bought (Art. 6(1)(b)); quota counters and abuse prevention are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the free service available (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Service providers (processors): Cloudflare, Inc. (server hosting and Workers AI) and Apple Inc. (App Store, subscriptions, iCloud). They process data only on our behalf or under their own terms with Apple.
  • International transfers: Cloudflare may process requests outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in Cloudflare's data processing terms.
  • Controller: Ha Cong Hau (Hà Công Hậu), Vietnam — arcanelle.support@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Export your data: Settings › Privacy › Export my data.
  • Delete your data: Settings › Privacy › Delete all my data, or delete the app.
  • Residents of the EU/EEA/UK (GDPR), Japan (APPI) and US states such as California (CCPA/CPRA) have rights to access, correct and delete personal data. We hold no information that identifies you (no name, email, Apple ID or reading content); the only server data are the pseudonymous counters described in section 5, which expire automatically. You can contact us to exercise your rights, and EU/EEA/UK residents may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Sensitive moments

If a question or note suggests you may be in crisis, the app shows support lines instead of a reading. This check runs only on your device.

9. Changes and contact

We will post changes here with a new effective date. Contact: arcanelle.support@gmail.com · Ha Cong Hau (Hà Công Hậu), Vietnam.
