FAQ

  • How do AI reflections work? On iPhones with Apple Intelligence they are written on your device. On other iPhones they use cloud AI only after you allow it; turn it off any time in Settings › AI reflections. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Do I need an account? No. Your subscription is linked to your Apple ID.
  • I reinstalled the app / changed phones and lost Premium. Open Settings › Premium › Restore purchases (same Apple ID).
  • How do I cancel? iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Arcanelle.
  • Refunds? Request at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Where is my journal stored? On your device. Use Settings › Privacy › Export my data to back it up.
  • How do I switch to Japanese? iPhone Settings › Arcanelle › Language.
